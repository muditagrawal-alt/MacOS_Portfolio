Engineering Philosophy

I value learning by building over purely memorizing theoretical concepts. The most instructive moments come when models break under edge cases, latencies spike on inference, or data pipelines hit unexpected bottlenecks.

Context Awareness: Tech is only as good as the problem it solves.
Robustness: Build systems that hold up outside clean sandbox environments.
